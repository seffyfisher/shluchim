You built a shaliach, it got stuck,
and there's no one to ask except the shaliach itself.
A bit like asking the suspect if he's guilty.

(Shluchim, singular shaliach, is Hebrew for "emissaries". It's what I call my AI agents.)


## Why I opened a group:

I build shluchim, and most of the time I'm alone.
When something gets stuck, I don't really have anyone to ask.

This blog helps me tell what I built.
But a blog is a one-way street.
I write, you read, and there's no conversation.

So I opened a small WhatsApp group.
For conversations between people who build, not another channel that blasts updates.


## Who it's for:

People building shluchim with Grok Bot, or similar tools.
You already built one, or you're in the middle.

It's a place to ask where you got stuck,
and to see what others actually built.
No hype, and no "look how amazing".


## And who it's less for:

If you're just curious and haven't built anything yet, totally fine.
The blog is here for you.

Come to the group when your first shaliach gets stuck.
And trust me, it will.


## How to join:

One note first: the group itself is in Hebrew.
People write in Hebrew.
I'm not inventing a separate English group.
If that works for you, you're welcome.

Join from this link:
https://chat.whatsapp.com/H1FJhw9lUCV8heZMsMjkM7

And in your first message, introduce yourself in three lines:
1. Who I am.
2. Which shaliach I built, or am building now.
3. Where I'm stuck.

And the third line is the entry ticket.

This isn't a course, and it isn't a support desk.
Just a few people who build, and ask each other.

See you there.
