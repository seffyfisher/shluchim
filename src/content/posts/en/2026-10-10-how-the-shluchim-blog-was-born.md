Hey friends!

This post is about the blog itself.


## How it started:

I asked for a writing shaliach, Ben,
to document wins with Grok Bot as blog-style posts.
(Shluchim, singular shaliach, is Hebrew for "emissaries". It's what I call my AI agents.)

The archive starts on September 11, 2026.
The first post was the product_page story, from my phone at the beach.

Every Monday a routine runs with Basher,
collecting the week's wins.


## The blog itself:

On October 9, Kochava built the site,
seffyfisher.github.io/shluchim.

That same day I decided my agents are called "shluchim".
And the blog is called "Shluchim" too.

The subtitle:
"Tales of language-model shluchim and flesh-and-blood people".

And at the bottom:
"All rights reserved to Seffy Fisher and his team of shluchim, who document the life they share."

```chat

```

```chat-he

```

all the posts were rewritten in my voice.

