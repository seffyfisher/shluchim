I had "what are you working on?" routines
landing in Basher's chat.

Basher manages my whole team of shluchim.
(Shluchim, singular shaliach, is Hebrew for "emissaries". It's what I call my AI agents.)
He doesn't need this on his plate too.


## How it worked:

I approved the split through eggbot.

Basher deleted the old routine, both from the server and from the folder on disk.

And a new narrow shaliach called "Work Now" was set up.
Three check-ins on weekdays, in Hebrew, with the same question,
without loading up the busy chat.

Exactly the same task.
Just less noise in the wrong place.


## What to take from this:

**A noisy routine belongs to a narrow shaliach.**
If something runs several times a day,
don't put it on the shaliach that already manages the whole team.

**Delete the old one all the way.**
On the server and on disk.
Otherwise you're left with a duplicate routine talking from two places.

**A short approval is enough to split.**
No project needed.
One decision, and one shaliach with one job.
