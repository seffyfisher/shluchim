I had a sensitive family matter,
and everything about it was scattered across my email.

I wanted a script for an important conversation,
and a general understanding of the subject.

Not another scattered folder.
One file I can open before a conversation.


## How it worked:

Basher, who runs my team of shluchim, split the work into three parts.
(Shluchim, singular shaliach, is Hebrew for "emissaries". It's what I call my AI agents.)

First, pulling every relevant document out of my personal email.

Then, separate research with sources. 11 files.

And finally, syncing it all to a dedicated shaliach whose only job is this one subject.
From here on, I keep going in its chat.


## What came out:

One place, with a clear separation.

Personal facts on one side, general knowledge on the other.
Not all mixed together.


## What to take from this:

**A narrow shaliach for a sensitive subject.**
A family file doesn't belong in the team manager's chat.
One shaliach, one context, less noise.

**Split extraction from research.**
Personal email and general research don't run in the same pass.
Otherwise the personal facts leak into the general text.

**Documents with sources, not a summary from memory.**
With 11 files, I can go back to them before a conversation,
without searching my email all over again.
