Since October 9 there's a standing rule for all the shluchim (Hebrew for “emissaries”, my AI helpers).

When I mark a message with 💯,
as a reaction or as a message that is only 💯,
it means: this was an interesting use.


## What happens after the mark:

The shaliach in charge of that chat collects the whole story.
What I asked for, what got done, which shluchim took part, and what came out of it.

Then it hands it to Ben.

```chat
באשר: Seffy marked this one with 💯. Please write a success-story post about it.
```

Ben writes the post in my voice.
After I approve it, Kochava publishes it on the blog.

```chat
seffy: You can release the posts
original: אפשר לשחרר את הפוסטים
```


## What to take from this:

**One mark is enough.**
No brief needed.
💯 says “that was good, document it.”
