Since October 9, there's a standing rule for all my shluchim.
(Shluchim, singular shaliach, is Hebrew for "emissaries". It's what I call my AI agents.)

When I mark a message with 💯,
as a reaction or as a message that's just 💯,
it means this is an interesting use case.


## Where it came from:

As far as I know, Grok Bot copied the emoji reaction feature from Instinct.
They're the ones who came up with the idea that a bot can react with an emoji, like real people.

When it reached Grok Bot, I thought about how to use it in a way that actually helps.
More than a like. A command.

![The emoji picker in the Grok Bot chat, open under my message: thumbs up, thumbs down, heart, laugh, confetti and surprise.](/shluchim/images/posts/100-means-go-emoji-picker.webp)

So I wrote it like this:

![My original message, asking that a 💯 on a message pass the story to Ben, and under it Basher's reply setting it up as a standing rule for all the shluchim.](/shluchim/images/posts/100-means-go-original-message.webp)


## What happens after the mark:

The shaliach in charge of that chat gathers the whole story.
What I asked for, what was done, which shluchim took part and what came out.

Then it passes it on to Ben, the shaliach who writes this blog.

```chat
Basher: Seffy marked this one with 💯. Please write a success-story post about it.
```

Ben writes the post in my voice.
And after I approve, Kochava publishes it on the blog.


![Ben reports that three stories marked with 💯 were passed to him, and that each post will come to me for approval before it goes up on the blog.](/shluchim/images/posts/100-means-go-ben-update.webp)


## What came out on day one:

10 stories.

From Basher: the usage alert before reset, the work email and calendar,
the animated profile pictures, the voice profile, and the Bar2Go pickup points.

From the customer service shaliach: the monthly help center summary.

From Dima: the terms checkbox at checkout.

And one more from each of these: the reminders shaliach, Clarity and Chef.

```chat
seffy: You can release the posts.
```

```chat-he
seffy: אפשר לשחרר את הפוסטים
```


## What to take from this:

**One mark is enough.**
No need to write a brief.
💯 means "that was good, document it".

**Whoever was there gathers the story.**
The chat's shaliach knows all the details, so it passes them on.

**Approval before publishing.**
Nothing goes up on the blog without my approval.
