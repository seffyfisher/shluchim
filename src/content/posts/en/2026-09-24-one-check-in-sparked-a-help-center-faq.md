I said I wanted to improve customer service.

Meaning: cross-check help.e-vrit.co.il against real tickets from Glassix,
our support system.
Find the gaps, and suggest new questions and answers.


## How it worked:

The "Work Now" check-in passed it to Basher, who runs my shluchim.
(Shluchim, singular shaliach, is Hebrew for "emissaries". It's what I call my AI agents.)

Basher didn't do the work himself.
He passed it to the customer service shaliach.

Back came a gap report, and 8 draft FAQs (frequently asked questions) in Hebrew.

The highlights:
"I paid and the book won't open" was weakly covered,
"marked delivered but never arrived" was missing completely,
and the "login paradox" was weakly covered.

Drafts only.
Nothing gets published without my approval.


## What came out:

From real pain on the ground to drafts ready for editing,
without me doing the cross-check myself.


## What to take from this:

**The team manager routes. He doesn't execute.**
Basher connects, and the voice-of-the-customer expert writes.
That way the busy chat doesn't fill up with content.

**A check-in can kick off real work.**
"What are you working on?" doesn't just log things.
It can set off a whole chain.

**Drafts in a file, not just in the chat.**
Something to open later, without digging through the conversation history.
