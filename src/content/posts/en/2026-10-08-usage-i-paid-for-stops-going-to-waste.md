I noticed I keep losing usage I already paid for.

The Grok Bot and Cursor quotas reset,
and I only find out after a lot of it went unused.

So on October 8, I asked Basher to warn me before the reset.
(Basher runs my shluchim. That's Hebrew for "emissaries", and it's what I call my AI agents.)


## How it worked:

Basher didn't guess.

First he sent a browser shaliach to the Grok Bot and Cursor dashboards.
It read the included usage and the reset dates from there.

Then Basher set up a routine called "Usage alert before reset".
It runs on Wednesday and Thursday at 10:20,
a day or two before the weekly reset, and also close to the monthly one.

Each run checks in the browser how much is left.
If a lot is left, it writes to me and suggests tasks from the backlog (the task queue) worth running.
If not, it stays quiet.


## Why this is excellent:

A "use it or lose it" quota turns into one reminder, right on time.

No noise. Real data from the dashboard.
And the leftover goes to real work from the backlog.


## What to take from this:

**First, read the real number.**
A browser shaliach on the dashboard beats any estimate.

**A good routine knows how to stay quiet.**
It only writes when there's a reason.

**Schedule by the reset, not by the clock.**
Wednesday and Thursday, while there's still time to use it.

**An alert comes with a suggestion.**
Along with "you have some left", it says "here's what's worth running".
