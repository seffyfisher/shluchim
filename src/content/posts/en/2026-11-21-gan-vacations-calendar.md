Kindergarten sent one photo, and with it, a whole evening of typing dates into the calendar.

![A girl holding a vacation schedule while her shaliach, a gray head wearing a hat of sticky notes, sends the dates across as colorful calendar events to a phone.](/images/posts/2026-11-21-gan-vacations-calendar-main-1536.webp)

Inside it was the vacation schedule of the afternoon program for the 2026-27 school year,
with dates going in every direction.

This is the first post in a new corner I'm opening here: **"AI and parents"**.
Small things from home, where AI does the annoying work for us.

(Shluchim, singular shaliach, is Hebrew for "emissaries". It's what I call my AI agents.)

![The vacation schedule of a kindergarten afternoon program, after removing identifying details](/images/posts/2026-11-21-gan-vacations-calendar-board-1580.webp)

## The problem:

In a table like this, "vacation" isn't always the same vacation.
On some days there's no kindergarten but there is afternoon care,
and on some there's nothing at all.

And that's exactly what you need to know at 07:00,
when you're deciding who stays home with the kid, and who has "an important meeting".

## What my wife did:

I was planning to sit down and type.
Every holiday, every range, from date to date.

Planning. That's the key word here.

She didn't wait for me (and rightly so).
She gave the photo to her shaliach (her AI agent),
and asked it to put everything into the family calendar.

She wrote to it:

> Put the details in my calendar starting from Hanukkah and not before because it's not relevant anymore, it's for my kindergarten. I want it marked if there's no afternoon program or there is

Then she reminded it that this goes in the family calendar, so it's shared.

The shaliach added 9 all-day events, from Hanukkah to Shavuot.
Days with afternoon care got ✅, and days without it got ❌.

![Screenshot of the chat with the shaliach: the photo from kindergarten, my wife's request, and the reply with the 9 calendar events, ✅ for days with afternoon care and ❌ for days without.](/images/posts/2026-11-21-gan-vacations-calendar-screenshot-735.webp)

For example, on Purim there's one day with no afternoon care,
and the next day it's fully open.
Exactly the detail I would have missed while typing, and found out about at 07:00.

The part I liked most:
family is our shared calendar.
That means it went in on her side, and I see it on mine,
without either of us typing a single date.
It's the closest we've gotten to a perfect split of the work at home.

## To copy:

Send your shaliach the photo from kindergarten as is, and paste:

```
Here's the kindergarten vacation schedule.
Add every vacation to our shared family calendar as an all-day event, from start date to end date.
Start from today onward, anything in the past isn't relevant.
In each event, mark whether there's afternoon care or not, and if there's an early pickup time, write it.
When you're done, send me a list of what you added, so I can check it against the photo.
```

## Variations, tips and pitfalls:

These are my tips, not things we've already tested in every variation:

- **A shared calendar, not a personal one.** If you don't name the calendar,
  it might land only with whoever asked.
- **A range, not a single day.** A five-day vacation is one event, not five.
- **What's past stays out.** It saves you extra events in the calendar.
- **Check once.** Open the calendar and go over the dates against the photo.
- **Same trick for school.** A message with what to bring and when, as a calendar reminder.
- **No shaliach connected to your calendar?** You can ask a regular AI chat to turn the photo into a calendar file (a file ending in .ics),
  and import it into Google Calendar.
